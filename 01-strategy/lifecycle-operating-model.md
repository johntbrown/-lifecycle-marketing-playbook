# Lifecycle Operating Model

## The Strategic View

Lifecycle marketing is the system that connects customer intent to long-term value.

A strong lifecycle program does not treat acquisition, onboarding, retention, and winback as separate channel programs. It treats them as one connected customer journey with shared data, shared definitions, and clear handoffs.

## The Lifecycle System

1. **Acquire the right customer**
   - Match message to need-state and source.
   - Optimize for customer quality, not conversion alone.

2. **Set expectations**
   - Clarify what the customer bought, how to use it, what happens next, and when value should become visible.

3. **Build the habit**
   - Attach the product or behavior to an existing routine.
   - Make consistency easier than remembering.

4. **Prove value**
   - Reinforce progress through outcomes, education, objective proof, and customer-specific milestones.

5. **Protect the rebill**
   - Surface billing, cadence, inventory, usage, and subscription controls before they become cancellation reasons.

6. **Deepen the relationship**
   - Use loyalty, personalization, cross-sell, education, community, and recognition to increase relevance over time.

7. **Recover the customer**
   - Diagnose why value broke down and use the right save, pause, product, cadence, or return path.

## Four Business Outcomes

Every lifecycle initiative should connect to at least one of these:

- **Revenue / Growth**: conversion, rebill, LTV, expansion
- **Risk**: churn, deliverability, compliance, customer confusion
- **Resources / Efficiency**: reusable systems, automation, operating leverage
- **Reputation**: trust, customer experience, brand consistency

## Decision Standard

Before launching any program, answer:

- What customer problem are we solving?
- Why does it matter to the business?
- What is the hypothesis?
- What decision will the result inform?
- What happens if the result is positive, negative, or inconclusive?

If those questions are unclear, the program is probably not ready to launch.
