# Onboarding Framework

## Objective

Move a new customer from purchase to confidence, consistency, and readiness for the next order.

## The Four Jobs of Onboarding

1. **Confirm the decision**
   - Reinforce what the customer bought and why it fits their goal.

2. **Set expectations**
   - Explain usage, timing, what progress may look like, and what not to over-interpret too early.

3. **Build the habit**
   - Help the customer connect usage to an existing routine.
   - Design for recovery when consistency breaks, not perfection.

4. **Prepare the rebill**
   - Make the next charge and shipment feel expected.
   - Surface subscription controls before frustration turns into churn.

## Journey Logic

Onboarding should respond to customer state, not just elapsed time.

Useful signals include:

- product purchased
- source / acquisition promise
- usage or engagement behavior
- subscription status
- skip / pause behavior
- progress feedback
- questions or support interactions

## Messaging Principles

- Clarity before cleverness.
- Progress before pressure.
- Reinforce consistency over perfection.
- Make expectations explicit.
- Do not assume the customer remembers what they bought or why.

## Success

A good onboarding program should improve more than opens and clicks. It should increase:

- first rebill readiness
- product confidence
- habit consistency
- subscription understanding
- downstream retention
