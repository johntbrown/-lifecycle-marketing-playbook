# Lifecycle Journey Audit Template

## 1. Business Goal
- What customer behavior are we trying to change?
- What business outcome does that behavior influence?

## 2. Audience
- Lifecycle state
- Product / plan
- Acquisition source
- Engagement state
- Known need-state or goal

## 3. Current Journey
For every touchpoint, document:
- timing / trigger
- channel
- audience
- message objective
- CTA
- destination
- exclusion logic
- owner

## 4. Customer Questions
At each step, ask:
- What does the customer need to know here?
- What are they likely worried about?
- What action should feel easiest next?
- What information do we already know that should change the experience?

## 5. Friction Audit
Look for:
- duplicate messages
- conflicting offers
- unclear expectations
- missing subscription controls
- broken links or destinations
- generic messaging despite known customer data
- over-contacting
- gaps around important customer decisions

## 6. Keep / Kill / Improve
Classify every touchpoint:

### Keep
Working, necessary, or strategically important.

### Kill
Redundant, low-value, confusing, or unsupported by a clear customer need.

### Improve
Useful purpose, weak execution.

## 7. Missing Moments
Identify lifecycle moments with no meaningful support.

Examples:
- pre-rebill
- post-delivery
- first sign of churn risk
- pause
- failed payment
- product buildup
- progress checkpoint
- review / referral moment

## 8. Measurement
Define:
- primary outcome
- diagnostic engagement metrics
- guardrails
- cohort definition
- attribution / observation window

## 9. Recommendation
End with:
1. What matters most
2. What should change
3. What should ship first
4. What needs testing
5. What can wait
