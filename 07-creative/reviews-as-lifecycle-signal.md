# Reviews as a Lifecycle Signal

## Thesis

Reviews should not be treated only as static social proof. A review is also a high-value customer signal that can trigger the next best lifecycle action.

## Better Review Flow

Instead of:

**purchase → generic review request → thank you**

Build:

**purchase → low-friction review request → rating / sentiment captured → follow-up based on response → reusable proof + customer insight**

## Reduce Friction

Where the technology supports it, let customers submit the review directly inside the email rather than forcing a secondary landing page.

Benefits:
- fewer steps
- higher completion potential
- clearer attribution
- easier segmentation by review behavior

## Positive Reviewer Path

A strong positive review can trigger:
- referral ask
- UGC request
- deeper outcome survey
- loyalty recognition
- product recommendation
- community invitation

## Neutral / Negative Reviewer Path

Do not immediately route every low score into more marketing.

Potential next actions:
- support outreach
- product education
- expectation reset
- product-fit diagnosis
- feedback capture

## Structured Outcome Data

A review program becomes more valuable when it captures structured follow-up data such as:
- use case
- goal
- perceived progress
- product used
- time to perceived value
- reason for recommending

This turns review collection into a source of zero-party data that can improve segmentation and future messaging.

## Measurement

Track:
- review request completion
- rating distribution
- review-to-referral rate
- review-to-UGC rate
- downstream retention by reviewer type
- support recovery for negative reviews

## Principle

The best review system creates three assets at once: customer proof, customer intelligence, and a new lifecycle trigger.
