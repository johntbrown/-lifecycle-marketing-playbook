# First 30-Day Acquisition Lifecycle

## Thesis

The first 30 days after lead capture are when intent is highest. The goal is not to send more messages. The goal is to convert the right prospects before intent decays, without training them to wait for discounts or damaging deliverability.

## Operating Model

**Measure → Segment → Optimize → Test → Scale**

### 1. Measure
Lock definitions before optimizing:
- What counts as a lead?
- What counts as a qualified lead?
- What counts as a first-time customer?
- What is the attribution window?
- What is the primary conversion event?

### 2. Segment
Use mutually exclusive lifecycle cohorts wherever possible:
- Day 0–7: new lead
- Day 8–14: active consideration
- Day 15–30: late-stage nurture
- Day 31+: engaged non-converter
- Dormant / unengaged

Layer in:
- acquisition source
- product / problem interest
- behavior
- consent status
- engagement depth

Behavior should outrank static time when the signal is strong enough. A person who browses product twice, completes a quiz, or returns to checkout should not be treated the same as a passive day-12 lead.

### 3. Message by decision barrier
Common territories:
- problem recognition
- education
- clinical / expert proof
- product differentiation
- social proof
- objection handling
- value
- urgency
- offer

The question is not “what email comes next?” It is “what does this person still need to believe before they buy?”

### 4. Coordinate Email + SMS
Treat channels as a system, not independent calendars.

Questions to answer:
- Does SMS add incremental conversion or just attribution?
- Which behavior should trigger SMS?
- What is the contact-pressure ceiling?
- Which messages are worth interrupting someone for?

A useful early test is Email-only vs Email + SMS with a clean holdout.

### 5. Use campaigns as a learning engine
Campaigns can test messaging territories faster than rebuilding flows every week.

Process:
1. Test creative/message territory in campaign sends.
2. Identify repeatable winners.
3. Convert winners into reusable content blocks.
4. Move durable learning into evergreen flows.

This creates a learning loop instead of a campaign treadmill.

## Key Metrics

Primary:
- 30-day lead-to-first-order conversion
- first-time customer conversion
- new subscription conversion
- revenue per lead

Secondary:
- time to first order
- Email vs SMS contribution
- product mix
- offer dependency
- downstream repeat / retention by acquisition source

## Guardrails

Do not optimize acquisition lifecycle in isolation.

A higher conversion rate is not automatically better if it produces:
- lower downstream retention
- higher refund rates
- higher churn
- stronger discount dependency
- poor customer-product fit

The best acquisition lifecycle creates customers who both convert and stick.
