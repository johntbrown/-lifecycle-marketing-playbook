# Lifecycle Segmentation Framework

## Thesis

Segmentation should change what the customer sees, when they see it, or what action the business takes. If a segment does not change the experience, it is probably reporting, not strategy.

A strong lifecycle segmentation model combines customer state, intent, need-state, behavior, product ownership, engagement, and value potential.

## Segmentation Hierarchy

Use segmentation in layers rather than forcing every business question into one field.

### 1. Relationship State
Examples:
- Lead
- One-Time Purchaser
- Active Subscriber
- Paused Subscriber
- Former Subscriber
- Lapsed Purchaser

This tells us where the customer is in the relationship.

### 2. Need-State / Problem to Solve
Examples:
- proactive health improvement
- symptom or problem-led interest
- measurable outcome / lab-driven goal
- convenience / routine support
- price / value sensitivity

This tells us why the customer is here.

### 3. Product Interest / Ownership
Track:
- product viewed
- product purchased
- current subscription
- products previously owned
- cross-sell eligibility

This tells us what is relevant now.

### 4. Intent / Behavior
Examples:
- new lead
- repeat site visitor
- quiz completer
- product-page browser
- checkout starter
- offer clicker
- account login
- subscription change

This tells us what the customer is doing now.

### 5. Engagement State
Examples:
- highly engaged
- recently engaged
- passive
- dormant
- never engaged

This tells us how aggressively the channel should communicate.

### 6. Risk / Opportunity
Examples:
- high conversion intent
- early churn risk
- product buildup risk
- dunning risk
- cross-sell ready
- winback ready

This tells us what the business should do next.

## Segmentation Rule

The best segmentation answers:

> What do we know about this customer that should materially change the next message, offer, or experience?

## Avoid Over-Segmentation

Do not create dozens of segments simply because the data exists.

Prefer a small number of durable dimensions that can be recombined:
- state
- intent
- need-state
- product
- engagement
- risk

## Principle

Good segmentation makes the customer experience more relevant and the operating model simpler at the same time.
