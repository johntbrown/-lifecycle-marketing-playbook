# Lifecycle Marketing Playbook

A practical framework for building lifecycle marketing as a growth system, not a collection of campaigns.

## Core Thesis

Lifecycle marketing should move a customer through a connected sequence:

**Acquire the right customer → Set expectations → Build the habit → Prove value → Protect the rebill → Deepen the relationship → Recover the customer when they leave**

The job is not simply to send more email or SMS. The job is to reduce friction, improve customer readiness, strengthen product fit, and create measurable business impact across the full customer journey.

## What This Playbook Covers

- **Strategy**: how to think about lifecycle as a business system
- **Acquisition**: lead conversion, segmentation, messaging, and channel orchestration
- **Onboarding**: expectation setting, habit formation, and early value realization
- **Retention**: rebill protection, value reinforcement, subscription fit, and loyalty
- **Winback**: cancellation recovery, reactivation, and return-to-value journeys
- **Measurement**: cohort thinking, incrementality, testing standards, and source-of-truth design
- **Templates**: reusable briefs, test plans, journey audits, and leadership updates

## Operating Principles

1. Start with the business question, not the channel.
2. Segment based on customer state and intent, not only time since signup.
3. Treat creative, data, product, and lifecycle as one operating system.
4. Every test should have a clear hypothesis, decision, and next action.
5. Measure downstream customer quality, not just top-of-funnel conversion.
6. Build systems that make good execution repeatable.
7. Use customer behavior to decide what happens next.
